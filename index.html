Suvankar Sen

I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), South Korea, where I started my doctoral research in September 2026.

My research interests lie at the intersection of metasurfaces, inverse design, optical materials, hyperspectral imaging and computational optics. I am interested in using computational and experimental approaches to develop new optical and optoelectronic systems.

01 — ABOUT

Researcher.
Engineer. Creator.

My background spans electrical engineering, nanotechnology, optoelectronics and experimental materials research.

My academic journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Biju Patnaik University of Technology, followed by a Master's degree in Nanotechnology from Vellore Institute of Technology.

During my research career I have worked with nanomaterials, perovskite solar cells, phase-change materials, thin films and optoelectronic devices.

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Electrical Engineering at UNIST in South Korea, beginning in September 2026.

My current interests are moving toward computationally designed optical systems, particularly metasurfaces, inverse design, hyperspectral imaging and related meta-optical technologies.

02 — RESEARCH

Metasurfaces

⌁

Inverse Design
